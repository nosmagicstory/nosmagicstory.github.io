Content Board

An internal content calendar and publishing tool used by the Magic Story team.

What it does

Content Board helps the Magic Story team plan, review and publish videos for our own brand accounts, such as The Defender Series (@thedefenderseries) on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

  • Plan posts on a weekly and monthly calendar.
  • Keep each video, its cover and its caption together in one place.
  • A team member approves every post before it is published. Nothing is posted without that approval.
  • After approval, Content Board uploads the finished video to our own account through each platform's official API and records the link.

Who can use it

Content Board runs on the Magic Story team's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and it only connects to accounts that Magic Story owns or manages.

How accounts are connected

The account owner signs in with the platform's official login page (for example, "Login with TikTok") and authorizes Content Board. Access tokens are stored only in the operating system's secure keychain on that computer. They are never sent to any other server.
