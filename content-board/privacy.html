Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information Content Board ("the app"), operated by Magic Story, uses and how it is protected.

1. Who uses the app

The app is an internal tool. It is used only by the Magic Story team to publish content to social media accounts that Magic Story owns or manages. It is not available to the public.

2. Information we use

  • Account information from the platform: the connected account's basic profile, such as its ID (for TikTok, the open_id), display name and avatar. We use this only to confirm that a post goes to the correct account.
  • Access tokens: the tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube when the account owner authorizes the app.
  • Our own content: the videos, covers, captions and schedules that the Magic Story team creates.
  • Post results: the post ID, link and status returned by the platform after publishing.

3. What we do not collect

The app does not collect, read or store data about other users, followers, viewers or their content. It does not use any data for advertising or profiling.

4. How information is stored and protected

  • Access tokens are stored only in the operating system's secure keychain on the team's computer. They are never written to files, logs or any external server.
  • Content and post records are stored on the team's own computer and in Magic Story's own cloud storage.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Information is sent only to the platform that a post is published to (for example, the video and caption are sent to TikTok when posting to TikTok).

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account owner disconnects the app or the token expires. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). On request, we delete the stored tokens and account information within 7 days.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: nos@magicstory.com
