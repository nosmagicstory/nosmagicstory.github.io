Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Content Board is an internal tool used only by the Magic Story team for accounts that Magic Story owns or manages. It stores only the access token issued by the platform and the connected account's basic profile (ID and name). It does not store data about other users.

How to remove Content Board's access

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business Integrations) → select Content Board Magic Story → Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Active → Content Board Magic Story → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions → Content Board Magic Story → Remove access.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Content Board → Remove access.

Removing access stops the app from using your account right away.

How to ask us to delete your data

Email nos@magicstory.com with the subject "Content Board data deletion" and the account name. We delete the stored token and account information within 7 days and confirm by email.

Content Board is operated by Magic Story (magicstory.com). Contact: nos@magicstory.com
